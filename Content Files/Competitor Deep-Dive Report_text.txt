1. Executive Summary

No competitor covers Campuna’s full range. Each one wins exactly one slice of the market with one repeatable page pattern: campertrader wins campervan/minicamper with type-PLPs plus a value-guide and tools; caraworld wins vehicles at scale with brand, model, price-band and Aufbauart PLPs; vanboerse wins the self-built-van niche from a single page; womo-gebraucht survives on the "von privat" angle; caravanmarkt owns dealer and service directory queries. Campuna’s opportunity is to combine these proven patterns on one domain: type PLPs + brand PLPs + price-band PLPs + a Wert/verkaufen money page + a service-provider directory — backed by categories none of them have (Zelte, Zubehör, Tiny Houses, Boote).

Keyword-gap result: of 151 top competitor rankings analysed, 91 are CONTENT GAPS not yet in Campuna’s master plan (mostly brand+model and campervan-synonym terms), 34 are already in the plan and now SERP-proven, 14 are navigational noise. The single biggest addition: a Wohnwagen brand-page set and a Campervan/Campingbus synonym cluster.

2.6 second.place (client-named reference)

Invite-only used camping & outdoor marketplace (Wohnmobile, Wohnwagen, Zubehör, Ausrüstung). Free for private; Bronze/Silver/Gold dealer packages; two-sided reviews; radius search. SEO footprint ≈ zero (DA 3, 2 keywords, ~0 visits — ranks only for "proverda" via a dealer page and "restposten camping" pos 44). Verdict: same model as Campuna but invisible — not a threat; a warning that community features alone don’t create search demand. Their /haendler/{name} profile pages are the one pattern worth noting — consistent with the caravanmarkt takeaway.


3. Cross-Competitor Patterns

Table stakes — every serious player has them (Campuna must match):

•    Type-level PLPs with real copy (H1, intro, FAQ) on top of the listing grid — campertrader & caraworld both rank with these

•    Free or low-friction private listing flow, stated loudly on the page

•    Entity pages for sellers/dealers/providers that catch name searches

Structural gaps across ALL five (Campuna wins here):

•    Nobody combines vehicles + gear + pitches + services + tiny houses + boats on one domain — Campuna’s category breadth is unique

•    Nobody has FAQPage/AEO-structured answers except caraworld’s cost block — the AI-answer space is open

•    No competitor has a modern Wert-Rechner + Ankauf funnel (campertrader has a guide only) — the €10–15-CPC sell cluster is weakly defended

•    Wohnwagen brand/model coverage is caraworld-only — a second player fits

•    Zubehör/Zelte/Dachzelt commerce SEO is owned by shops & Kleinanzeigen, not by any marketplace — Campuna’s Flohmarkt equivalent can take it
