8. Language rules for all future German copy
Pure, natural German, informal du address, short sentences, active voice
No dashes in body copy. Use comma, colon or full stop instead. Hyphens inside compound words (Camping-Marktplatz) are correct German and stay.
No marketing fluff, no exclamation chains, no fake urgency
Numbers and facts only when verifiable. Never invent anything.
Place the keyword naturally: Camping-Marktplatz in H1, first paragraph and footer. Do not stuff.
