•    Place the keyword naturally: Camping-Marktplatz in H1, first paragraph and footer. Do not stuff.

3. Category PLP rules (apply to all 9 categories)

Canonical: every filter, sort and page parameter (?sortierung=, ?preis=, ?zustand=, ?seite=) canonicalises to the base category URL. Exception: ?seite=2 and higher are self-canonical with title suffix "| Seite 2". Never noindex pagination.

Category navigation is never a filter parameter: a category click goes to the real category URL (/zubehoer, /wohnmobile ...), never to /inserate?kategorie=zubehoer. If parameter URLs exist for technical reasons they 301 to the clean URL.

Subcategory chips and Beliebte Suchen chips are real <a href> links to indexable PLPs. No onclick-only navigation anywhere in the category system.

Load-more needs the crawlable fallback link to ?seite=2. Every paginated page links to the next page. Listing detail URLs on page 2+ must be reachable as plain links.

Count in the results header equals the number of rendered cards. Test listings, drafts and sold/deleted listings never render and their URLs return 404 or 410 (sold: 301 to category after 30 days is also fine, pick one and keep it).

Empty category: page stays live and indexable with the empty-state message, sell CTA and full SEO text block. Never redirect, never noindex an empty category.

No Product schema on category pages (Google guideline: no Product markup on category/list pages). Allowed stack: BreadcrumbList + CollectionPage with ItemList + FAQPage.

ItemList in CollectionPage schema is generated dynamically from the first page of listings: position, name, url per item. Cap at the visible page size. The 3 items in the template are examples of the exact shape.

One H1 per page. Subcategory PLPs get their own H1/title/description following the same pattern (example: Vorzelte & Markisen gebraucht kaufen | Campuna).

All visible copy pure human German, du-form. No em dashes, no en dashes anywhere. Hyphens in compound words (Camping-Zubehör, Camping-Marktplatz) are correct German and stay.
