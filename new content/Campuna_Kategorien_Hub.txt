0. Structure analysis of the approved design

The approved design is a homepage section: eyebrow "KATEGORIEN", the headline "Camping hat viele Seiten. Wir bringen sie zusammen." and 9 icon cards. As a standalone hub page that is thin content: 9 icons with 2-word labels give Google almost no text and give the category pages almost no anchor context. The following sections were ADDED. Build all of them.

A. Intro paragraph under the H1

Two sentences of context with the primary entities (Kategorien, Camping, ganz Deutschland, kostenlos inserieren). Keeps the hero visually identical, adds crawlable text.

B. Breadcrumb + BreadcrumbList schema

Startseite › Kategorien. Two levels, visible and in schema.

C. Category detail blocks (the SEO core)

One block per category: small tag, H2 with the category name linked, 45 to 60 words of keyword copy with an in-text deep link, subcategory chips, "Zur Kategorie" button. This turns the hub into 9 rich, descriptive internal links instead of 9 icons, and creates crawl paths to every subcategory PLP.

D. Stats strip

9 Kategorien · ganz Deutschland · private Inserate kostenlos, ohne Provision · direkter Kontakt. Trust + entity reinforcement directly under the cards.

E. Beliebte Suchen

8 chips with the highest-volume cross-site searches, each a deep link (wohnmobil gebraucht kaufen, vorzelt, dachzelt, tiny house kaufen, kastenwagen, stellplatz, gasprüfung, fahrradträger).

F. Sell CTA band

The hub also serves sellers choosing where to list. Dark green band with "Kostenlos inserieren".

G. FAQ + FAQPage schema

4 hub questions (which categories exist, where does my listing belong, is listing free, Marktplatz vs. Kategorien). Visible text matches schema word for word. First item open by default.

Fixed from the design

Card labels "Camping Zubehör" and "Camping Services" are written with hyphen (Camping-Zubehör, Camping-Services): correct German orthography and consistent with the category page H1s and the footer. Emoji icons in the template are placeholders: replace with the final SVG line icon set, aria-hidden="true", labels stay real text.

3. Hub page rules


Every card and every CTA links to the clean category URL (/zubehoer, /wohnmobile, ...). Never to /inserate?kategorie=... and never JS-only navigation.


The page canonicals to itself (https://campuna.de/kategorien). No parameters exist on this page.


One H1 (the design headline). Category names are H2. FAQ questions are H3.


Listing counts, when shown, come from the database at render time and equal reality. Omit when unavailable.


Subcategory chips link to subcategory PLPs; a chip whose PLP is not live yet points to the parent category instead. No link on this page may 404.


Emoji icons are placeholders for the final SVG icon set (aria-hidden="true"). Labels stay real text, never text in images.


Header marks Kategorien as active. This page is linked from the main navigation on every page: it is the strongest internal-link distributor after the homepage. Keep it in the top nav.


All visible copy pure human German, du-form. No em dashes, no en dashes. Hyphens in compounds (Camping-Zubehör, Camping-Services, Camping-Marktplatz) are correct and stay.

5. Pre-launch verification checklist


Title 60 max, description 155 max, exactly one H1 (this template: 46 / 148 / 1).


Zero em dashes and zero en dashes in visible copy (checked: 0).


All 3 JSON-LD blocks parse (validator.schema.org + Google Rich Results Test); FAQ text matches visible text verbatim.


All 9 category links resolve 200 to the clean URLs; subcategory chips resolve 200 (parent fallback where sub-PLP not live).


Breadcrumb visible and matching schema.


Mobile: cards wrap 5/3/2 columns, detail blocks stack, tap targets 44px min, no horizontal scroll.


Icons replaced with final SVG set, aria-hidden, labels as text.


Page reachable from the top navigation sitewide and from the homepage KATEGORIEN section headline.
